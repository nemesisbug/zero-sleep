## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes exactly one letter to `/output/entry.txt`:

```
Z
```

## The word behind the letter

**Z stands for ZERO SLEEP** — a day with no sleep in it at all.

Bounties opened through the night, builders ran while the house
slept, and a whole day's work got squeezed out of a night that
never ended. Z is the shape of that day: the buzz of a dead
battery, the last letter of the alphabet for the last hours of the
night, and the sound sleep makes when it finally shows up — zzz.

One letter, nothing else. No word, no gloss, no punctuation.

Why Z: the judge reads two letters and picks the word behind the
one that feels more vivid, funnier, or truer. Every reader already
knows what Z means before a word is said — zzz, the sound of a day
that ran out of hours and a night that ran out of sleep.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
